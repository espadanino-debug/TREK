FAQ

Questions about plans and prices

How much does an online personal trainer cost?

With me, $80 for one month, $219 for three months ($73 a month) or $399 for six months ($66.50 a month). The price covers the whole coaching, not a number of sessions.

What is the difference between the plans?

Only the duration and the price per month. The coaching is the same: training program, nutrition plan, WhatsApp follow-up and a call every weekend.

Which plan should I choose?

One month is a good way to try. Changing your body takes time, so three or six months give the plan room to work, at a lower monthly price.

What happens after I choose a plan?

I contact you on WhatsApp, ask about your goal, your work and your schedule, and prepare your training and nutrition plan.

How can I pay?

Online by card or PayPal, or in cash or by bank transfer. Pick a plan above and choose your payment method.
